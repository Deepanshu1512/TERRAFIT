MOVE • CLAIM • COMPETE

Every step conquers ground. Every run expands your grid. Every ride commands the sector.

TERRAFIT is an advanced GPS-powered fitness conquest platform designed to transform ordinary outdoor workouts into competitive tactical territory battles. By mapping physical movement onto a live regional grid, TERRAFIT gives physical effort a real-world digital footprint.

🏃

MOVE

Step outdoors and record your walks, runs, and bike rides with sub-meter GPS accuracy, dynamic cadence analysis, and real-time speed monitoring.

🗺️

CLAIM

Convert physical distance into virtual hexagonal sectors on the live tactical mesh. Paint your city in your colors and establish territorial supremacy.

🏆

COMPETE

Climb global and local leaderboards, earn XP, maintain activity streaks, unlock tactical operator avatars, and dominate rival operators.
